FindMyPark

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

FindMyPark (“the app”, “we”, “us”) helps you save and find your parking spot. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and who else it reaches.

Contact: nerlybiton@gmail.com

  • Your saved parking spot is stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we never receive, see, or store your location.
  • Location data is sent to Google so the map can be drawn and an address looked up.
  • The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, which uses an advertising identifier.
  • Buying FindMyPark Pro is handled by Google Play and RevenueCat; we never see your payment details.
  • You can delete everything the app holds at any time, from inside the app.

What the app collects and why

Location

WhatWhenWhy

Approximate location Same Fallback when GPS is unavailable
Background location (Pro only) Only if you enable auto-detect and grant “Allow all the time” To record your parking spot automatically when your car’s Bluetooth disconnects

Your saved parking spot — coordinates, street address, your note, and any meter timer — is written to your device’s local app storage. It is not uploaded anywhere. It stays on the device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Bluetooth (Pro only)

If you enable auto-detect, the app observes whether your phone’s Bluetooth radio is on or off, so it can notice when you disconnect from your car. It does not read your paired device list, device names, or any content exchanged over Bluetooth.

Advertising

The free version shows banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and basic usage signals in order to serve and measure ads. We request non-personalised ads by default.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form before any ad loads, and no ad is requested until you have made a choice. You can change that choice later from within the app.

Purchases

FindMyPark Pro is sold through Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user identifier and your subscription status so the app knows whether Pro is active. We never receive or store your card details, billing address, or any other payment information — that stays between you and Google.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, sign-up, email address, or name
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No location history — only your single most recent saved spot

Your choices and rights

Location. You can refuse or revoke location access at any time in Settings → Apps → FindMyPark → Permissions. The app still works — you can place your parking pin by tapping the map instead of using GPS. Background location can be revoked separately without affecting anything else.

Ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Buying Pro removes ads entirely. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you can withdraw or change your ad consent from within the app.

Deleting your data. Tap Delete in the app to remove your saved parking spot. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored locally. Because we hold nothing on a server, there is nothing further for us to delete — but if you have questions, write to the address at the top of this policy.

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Since the app stores your data only on your own device, you can exercise most of these directly. For data held by the third parties above, please contact them using the links in the table.

If you are in California, the CCPA/CPRA gives you the right to know what personal information is collected and to request its deletion. We do not sell personal information as that term is defined under the CCPA.

Children

FindMyPark is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data retention and security

Your parking data lives in your device’s private app storage, protected by Android’s per-app sandbox, and is retained until you delete it or uninstall the app. No transfer over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, but since the app does not transmit your parking data at all, the exposure here is limited to your own device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will always reflect the current version, and material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play.
